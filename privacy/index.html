Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Local In-Browser Processing (No Data Sent to Our Servers)

Your privacy and your children's privacy are fundamental to how BrightWorkSheets was engineered. All worksheet creation tools—including name tracing, alphabet tracing, number tracing, math worksheet generators, word search puzzles, and mazes—run 100% locally in your web browser (client-side JavaScript).

Any personal names, custom words, seed numbers, or worksheet options you type are processed entirely on your own computer, tablet, or phone. They are never transmitted, collected, logged, or stored on any external servers or databases.

2. Third-Party Advertising and Cookies

To keep our learning tools free for all families, teachers, and classrooms, we may display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdSense). These third-party vendors and ad networks may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Automatically Collected Information & Analytics

Like most websites, our web hosting server and standard analytics services may automatically record standard technical metrics when you access our site, such as your browser user-agent, operating system, referring URL, and timestamps. This data is used exclusively to diagnose server performance and improve user experience.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

BrightWorkSheets is designed for use by adults—specifically parents, guardians, teachers, and homeschool educators. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Because all custom text remains strictly in the user's browser, no child's name or personal identifier is ever received by us.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external educational resources or third-party services. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites.

6. Your Rights and Contacting Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our practices, please email us directly at: contact@brightworksheets.com.
